Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any of the personal information you enter into the Pulse CV builder on our servers. All data is processed locally in your browser.

2. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage feature to save your progress so you can return to your CV later. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser data or using the "Reset App" button.

3. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
